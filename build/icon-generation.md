## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production macOS application icon for Folio, a quiet Japanese Markdown reader. Create one beautiful finished square app icon, 1024 by 1024 composition. A softly rounded macOS squircle tile in rich muted forest/sage green (#577458), centered on a truly transparent canvas with a small even transparent margin. On the tile, a bold sculptural warm ivory open book with two gently curved paper pages and a clear central fold; restrained paper layering and soft dimensional shadows, elegant precision, calm premium desktop productivity app feeling. Integrate a small minimalist downward arrow embossed subtly on the right page to suggest Markdown, without adding clutter. Front-facing, balanced symmetric silhouette, strong legibility at 32 px, minimal details, subtle upper-left studio light, rounded edges, understated depth. No lettering, no wordmark, no watermark, no extra objects, no surrounding scene. Actual alpha transparency outside the rounded icon tile; not a checkerboard picture. Deliver just the single icon.
